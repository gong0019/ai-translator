## SCOPE
CHINESE → RUSSIAN

## GRAMMAR
1. Assign Russian case and preposition to every noun phrase from its explicit Chinese semantic role.
2. Give every finite Russian clause an explicit or grammatically encoded subject derived from the Chinese referent.
3. Maintain gender, number, adjective, pronoun, and past-tense agreement.
4. Use perfective aspect for a bounded completed event and imperfective aspect for an ongoing, habitual, repeated, or unbounded event.
