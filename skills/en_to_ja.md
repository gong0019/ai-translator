## SCOPE
ENGLISH → JAPANESE

## GRAMMAR
1. Place every predicate at the end of its Japanese clause and assign は, が, を, に, で, と, and へ from the source semantic role.
2. Preserve every explicit agent, patient, recipient, cause, condition, comparison, and negation even when Japanese omits a repeated subject.
3. Use one register throughout the input. Use です・ます unless the source is a headline, code label, quotation with a different register, or formal document clause.
4. Convert English relative clauses into Japanese prenominal clauses without losing the modified noun or any clause argument.
