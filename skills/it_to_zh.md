## SCOPE
ITALIAN → SIMPLIFIED CHINESE

## GRAMMAR
1. Recover a grammatically omitted Italian subject from verb morphology and the nearest explicit antecedent. Do not create an absent referent.
2. Resolve every direct, indirect, reflexive, partitive, and combined clitic to its verb and referent before translation.
3. Convert flexible word order and subordinate clauses into explicit Chinese semantic roles without deleting modifiers.
4. Preserve indicative, subjunctive, conditional, imperative, tense, aspect, modality, negation, and uncertainty.
