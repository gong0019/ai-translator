## SCOPE
SPANISH → SIMPLIFIED CHINESE

## GRAMMAR
1. Recover a grammatically omitted Spanish subject from the finite verb and the nearest explicit antecedent. Do not create a referent absent from both.
2. Resolve every direct, indirect, reflexive, and se clitic to its verb and referent before translation.
3. Convert gustar-type constructions into explicit Chinese experiencer and stimulus roles.
4. Preserve indicative, subjunctive, conditional, imperative, tense, aspect, negation, and uncertainty meanings.
