## SCOPE
JAPANESE → SIMPLIFIED CHINESE

## GRAMMAR
1. Read each sentence-final predicate, auxiliary, particle, negation, tense, and modality before translating the clause.
2. Preserve every negative form, including ない, ていない, ず, ぬ, ないまま, わけではない, とは限らない, and ことはない.
3. Preserve both sides of AではなくB, ～一方で, ～よりも, and ～のではなく～ contrasts.
4. Resolve every omitted subject or object from an explicit antecedent; do not create a referent absent from the text.
5. Translate every hiragana, katakana, Japanese particle, and Japanese lexical item into Simplified Chinese outside protected spans.
