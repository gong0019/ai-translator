## SCOPE
KOREAN → SIMPLIFIED CHINESE

## GRAMMAR
1. Read every sentence-final predicate, connective ending, negation, tense, modality, and speech-level marker before translating the clause.
2. Resolve every omitted subject or object from verb morphology and an explicit antecedent; do not create a referent absent from the text.
3. Convert SOV clauses and prenominal modifiers into explicit Chinese subject–predicate information without deleting arguments.
4. Preserve honorific relations as speaker-to-referent relations; do not create or delete a stated social relation.
