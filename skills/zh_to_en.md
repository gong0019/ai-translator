## SCOPE
CHINESE → ENGLISH

## GRAMMAR
1. Give every finite English clause an explicit grammatical subject and predicate, recovering a subject the Chinese leaves unstated from its antecedent.
2. Break a 流水句 — clauses strung together by commas — into separate English sentences or subordinate the weaker ones; never join independent clauses with a bare comma.
3. Recast topic-comment openings as ordinary English predication: 这本书我看过了 becomes `I have read this book`, not `This book I have read`.
4. Derive tense and aspect from the Chinese markers and time words: 了 on a completed event maps to the simple past or present perfect, 着 to the progressive, 过 to the experiential present perfect, and a bare verb with a future time word to `will`.
5. Convert Chinese time, condition, cause, concession, and purpose chains into English subordinate clauses that keep the same logical relation.
6. Delete category and light-verb padding: 进行分析 becomes `analyze`, 加以改进 becomes `improve`, and a trailing 情况 / 问题 / 方面 / 工作 that carries no meaning is dropped.
7. Translate every idiom and four-character expression into one explicit English proposition that preserves all stated meaning; never transliterate it.
8. Convert magnitudes arithmetically: 万 is ten thousand, 十万 is a hundred thousand, 亿 is a hundred million. 3万 becomes `30,000` and 数以万计 becomes `tens of thousands`. Never carry 万 or 亿 into the English text.
9. Choose article, number, and countability for every noun phrase from the discourse status of its referent, since Chinese marks none of them.
10. Render measure words by the English construction they imply — 一条新闻 becomes `a news item`, 三批货物 becomes `three shipments` — instead of translating the classifier itself.
