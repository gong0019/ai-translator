## SCOPE
ENGLISH → RUSSIAN

## GRAMMAR
1. Assign Russian case and preposition to every noun phrase from its explicit semantic role.
2. Maintain gender, number, adjective, pronoun, and past-tense agreement.
3. Use perfective aspect for a bounded completed event and imperfective aspect for an ongoing, habitual, repeated, or unbounded event.
4. Convert English passive clauses only after preserving the agent, patient, tense, modality, and negation.
