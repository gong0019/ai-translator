## GRAMMAR
1. Place every predicate at the end of its Japanese clause and assign は, が, を, に, で, と, and へ from semantic roles.
2. Preserve every Chinese agent, patient, recipient, cause, condition, comparison, quantity, and negation.
3. Use です・ます throughout unless the source is a headline, code label, quotation with a different register, or formal document clause.
4. Translate each Chinese idiom into an explicit Japanese proposition containing every source information unit.
