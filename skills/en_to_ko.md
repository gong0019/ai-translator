## SCOPE
ENGLISH → KOREAN

## GRAMMAR
1. Place every predicate at the end of its Korean clause and assign subject, topic, object, location, direction, and instrument particles from source roles.
2. Preserve every explicit agent, patient, recipient, cause, condition, comparison, and negation.
3. Use one Korean speech level throughout each speaker’s text. Preserve explicit honorific relations and do not create unexpressed honorific relations.
4. Convert English relative clauses into Korean prenominal clauses without deleting the modified noun or a clause argument.
